Payment Desk help

Current scope: Michigan resident retail purchases. Automatic nationwide tax and registration rules are not connected. Other states, leases, nonresident or exempt purchases, manufacturer rebates and special registrations are blocked. Use your dealership’s approved systems for these transactions.

Make an estimate

  1. Open Deal details and check the vehicle reference, date, buyer registration state and transaction coverage.
  2. Enter selling price, purchase type, trade allowance/payoff and cash down. For finance, enter the assumed annual interest rate and term. The rate is not a lender APR disclosure.
  3. Review taxes and registration, dealership fees, and each optional product. Enter a new-registration cost when needed. A positive Other product needs a name and an explicit tax choice.
  4. Use Payment grid to compare terms, rates and down payments. Roll to a target previews adjustments; only Apply changes the deal. Verify lender, manager and product eligibility separately.
  5. Open Customer view and review the itemized estimate before exporting. Invalid, incomplete, unsupported or expired-rule estimates cannot be exported.

Send the actual PDF

When your browser supports sharing PDF files, Share PDF opens your device’s sharing menu with the customer PDF attached. Choose Messages or another available app, then review the recipient and send there. The app cannot confirm receipt. Cancelling does not send or download a file.

If file sharing is unavailable, use Download PDF, then attach that file in your messaging app. Copy summary remains a separate text action. Print uses the browser’s print screen. A PDF preparation error offers Retry.

Your device and dealership settings

Settings sets the optional dealership name/logo and the document/CRV fees in this browser profile. Reset deal clears the saved worksheet and leaves dealership settings. The website and Chrome companion keep separate worksheets and settings.

The current draft saves automatically when storage and browser locking are available. Refresh restores its figures and unfinished input. Check the footer for Saved, storage failure or another-tab conflicts. An unreadable draft is preserved until you explicitly discard it. An older app version cannot overwrite the new format.

On shared devices, use your own protected browser profile and reset when finished. Clear dealership settings separately if the device is changing dealerships. Downloads, clipboard content, printed copies and sent PDFs must be managed separately. See data handling.

Inventory companion

Install the packaged Payment Desk Companion folder containing manifest.json and the bundled desk folder. Do not load the extensions parent folder or source-only folder. Developer-mode updates require replacing the complete package and reloading the extension; Chrome controls its outer width.

In the companion, open Inventory, paste your dealership homepage or an unfiltered New/Used inventory page, then choose Connect and refresh. Chrome asks to read that dealership website on its www or non-www host; access stays scoped to those hosts. The catalog stays in this browser, and declining keeps the existing connection unchanged. If access is declined, allow the dealership website in Chrome’s extension settings and retry Connect and refresh; the entered URL remains available.

Some website platforms need a different connector. Retry with the public, unfiltered inventory page. If refresh cannot verify inventory, use Enter vehicle or Capture listing while connector support is evaluated. Do not bypass website restrictions. A partial refresh keeps previous vehicles and their last-seen dates; cached records are not a guarantee that a vehicle is available or sold. Review advertised price and conditions before importing.

After refresh, choose Web app ↗ in the companion once to connect its catalog to this Chrome profile. Return to Payment Desk → Inventory → Reload catalog. The web Inventory picker reads the public catalog, not the companion worksheet. If a catalog changes while paging, Reload catalog starts from the first page.

Compare two customer limits

In Roll to a target, enter the monthly payment target and open Payment + cash limits. The cash limit includes all cash due at signing, including negative equity paid upfront. Options use the entered term and rate assumptions with vehicle price, trade, products and fees fixed; estimated interest shows the cost of extending the loan. Apply an option explicitly and use Undo before the next edit. Leave the cash limit blank for the original one-part adjustments.

Grid rate edits change comparison assumptions only. Select a payment to change the worksheet. In Customer view, Adjust payment options returns to the grid. Missing-field actions take you straight to the required input. Cash clears financing down; a nonzero down entry exposes an Undo for that switch.

Payment/cash limits and independent comparison rates save with this device’s worksheet. An older app may preserve the new draft as unreadable; reload the current app instead of discarding it. Draft format 3 keeps earlier supported deal figures during migration.

Recover or report a problem

For another-tab draft conflicts, keep any needed figures from this page, then reload. For settings changed elsewhere, reload before comparing or sharing. When a broken worksheet offers Reset, saved-draft cleanup can still fail; check its warning. For a companion startup failure, use Retry or replace the complete package.

Give your deployment maintainer the app version/build shown in the footer, extension version, browser/OS, steps and a synthetic example. Do not include customer information, real worksheet figures, credentials or a full browser profile in a public issue. Commercial support contacts and response commitments must come from your deployment operator.

Data in this release

This technical notice describes v2.1.0. Your deployment operator supplies the applicable business privacy notice, contact and commercial terms. It does not promise encryption, regulated-system compliance or cloud backup.

Stored in this browser profile

  • One current worksheet: financial figures, vehicle reference, product names, selected date, grid/target values and unfinished numeric/date text. It is restored after refresh.
  • Dealership name/logo and fee settings, stored separately from the worksheet.
  • The public identifier of a connected Chrome companion. The companion separately stores its selected website, public inventory catalog and refresh state.

Storage is scoped to a browser profile and website or extension origin. Others using that profile can reopen it. The website and companion do not synchronize worksheets. Browser storage clearing, extension removal or device loss can erase data. A draft is not a backup or a saved-proposal library.

Requests and explicit sharing

The web host receives ordinary site requests and may retain hosting/access logs. App scripts and fonts are bundled locally; this release has no analytics SDK or shared-cloud worksheet backend. The companion makes permissioned requests to public dealership pages, robots files and inventory sources; viewing public vehicle images can request their image hosts.

Opening the web app from the companion connects the bounded public catalog in that Chrome profile. The bridge excludes private companion drafts and settings. Capturing/importing a listing transfers reviewed vehicle price/reference information. Copy, PDF download, sharing and printing happen only through the corresponding user action; copies then exist outside the app. The app cannot erase a recipient’s attachment or confirm message delivery.

No automatic nationwide tax provider is connected. A future provider would require a separate, accurate disclosure of required transaction/location facts, processing and retention before those requests are enabled.

Clear after use

Reset deal attempts to clear the saved worksheet, including migrated legacy figures. A small figures-free format marker prevents old data from reappearing. Check for storage/conflict warnings; failed cleanup is not successful deletion. Clear dealership settings separately. Disconnect inventory in the companion to remove its connected catalog.

An unreadable saved worksheet is kept until explicit discard. Browser site-data removal can also erase stored app records. Manage other open tabs, Downloads, clipboard, printouts, backups and sent attachments separately. Do not enter names, contact details, SSNs, bank details, credit reports or application data into free-text estimate fields.
